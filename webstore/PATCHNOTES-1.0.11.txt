What's new in Glossa 1.0.11

• Smoother hovering: Small movements over the same word no longer restart the translation delay.
• Fewer duplicate translation requests and more reliable switching between words.
• Outdated results are discarded when changing languages or turning Glossa off.
• Improved word detection on modern websites and better protection for input fields and editors.

Glossa stays lightweight: no new settings or additional permissions.
